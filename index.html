Email Organizer

A personal, keyboard-first Gmail client for iPhone, iPad and Mac, built for and used by one person (Ryan Waranauskas). It is not sold or offered to the public and is distributed only to its owner through Apple TestFlight.

What it does

  • Shows several Gmail inboxes, one account at a time, sorted into split inboxes (Important, Calendar, Other).
  • Keyboard shortcuts and swipe gestures for triage: done, snooze, reply, move.
  • Snooze, reminders when nobody replies, scheduled sending, and one-tap unsubscribe.
